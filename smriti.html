      स्मृति /smriti

      A personal notepad that lives in a single file — no server, no cloud, no install. Everything saves instantly in your browser. Open it from the ☀ menu bar or via open-smriti.sh.

      Up to four tabs, each with its own color and page style (ruled · grid · dot · blank). Switch to grid view to see all tabs at once. Each task can carry a countdown timer or a fixed deadline. Drag an image or paste a screenshot to pin it. Record a voice note with 🎙. Tick a task to archive it. Tab titles are editable — just click and type.

      completely private · zero footprint
      • No internet connection required. Works 100% offline, always.
      • No cloud, no sync, no accounts, no subscriptions. Nothing leaves your machine.
      • No tracking. No analytics. No cookies. No telemetry of any kind.
      • Your notes live only in your browser's local storage — invisible to everyone else.
      • The entire app is one HTML file. Open source code. Nothing hidden.
